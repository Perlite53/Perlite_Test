Overview

Discover a New Dimension of Living at Perlite Premier

Step into a thoughtfully designed residential community where modern living meets everyday convenience. Perlite Premier is located in the rapidly growing neighbourhood of Singena Agrahara, just minutes from Electronic City Phase 2, offering the perfect balance between connectivity and peaceful surroundings.
Spread across two elegant blocks, the project features spacious 2 and 3 BHK homes with smart layouts that enhance comfort, privacy, and functionality. With three-side open residences, private balconies, and no common walls, every home is designed to welcome abundant natural light and fresh air.
The development is planned with ample open spaces and landscaped areas, creating a refreshing environment for residents. A dedicated clubhouse and lifestyle amenities—including a swimming pool, indoor games, fitness spaces, and leisure zones—ensure there’s something for every member of the family.
Strategically positioned for excellent connectivity, the project is positioned for unmatched accessibility—near Huskur Metro, with quick reach to the upcoming PRR, and walkable access to the suburban rail network. It is also well-connected to major IT hubs, reputed schools, and healthcare facilities, ensuring everything you need is within easy reach. Built with a strong focus on quality, Vaastu-compliant design, and long-term value, Perlite Premier is not just a home—it’s a well-rounded lifestyle choice for modern urban living.
